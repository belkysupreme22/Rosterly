# Details

The small things that make this site feel finished. People notice them without naming them; apply the ones that fit your project.

- **Frosted glass**: 2 surfaces blur what's behind them (backdrop-filter), usually headers, menus and overlays.
- **Scroll snapping**: Scrolling settles on whole items or sections (scroll-snap).
- **Outlined text**: Some text is drawn as an outline (-webkit-text-stroke).
- **Balanced line breaks**: Headings and paragraphs wrap evenly (text-wrap: balance / pretty) on 140 elements: no lonely last words.
- **Considered underlines**: Links set their own underline offset rather than the browser default.
- **Shaped edges and fades**: 6 clip-path shapes and 15 masks cut or fade edges instead of hard rectangles.
- **3D CSS**: 9 elements use perspective or preserve-3d for depth without WebGL.
- **Grain texture**: A fine noise texture sits over surfaces (an SVG turbulence filter or noise image), so flat colour feels printed.
- **Focus ring**: Keyboard focus shows as rgb(255, 255, 255) (:focus-visible).
- **Styled scrollbars**: Scrollbars are restyled to match the interface.
