# Voice

Tone: concise, direct, calm, conversational.

- Keep sentences around 10 words; that is the measured average across the site's copy.
- Write headings in sentence case; only 4 of 21 measured headings use Title Case.
- Address the reader as "you" (about 3.3 times per 100 words on the site).
- Avoid exclamation marks; the measured copy almost never uses them.
- Label buttons with short nouns or phrases, about 3 words long, set in capitals.

## Examples (new sentences in this voice)

- **headline**: Plan less. Ship more.
- **button**: Start now
- **empty state**: Nothing here yet.
- **error**: That didn't work. Try again.
